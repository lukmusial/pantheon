# Eagle relief photos

Reference for the pediment's gilded bronze eagle (`assets/eagle/`, `tools/eagle_relief.py`): the
Trajanic relief of an eagle in an oak wreath with ribbons, 2nd century AD, probably from the Forum
of Trajan, set into the portico of Santi Apostoli, Rome, in 1474.

All four photos by Sailko, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/):
